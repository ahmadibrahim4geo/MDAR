## Summary
Authored `scripts/generate_excel_companion.ts` to generate an authentic Office Open XML (`.xlsx`) companion workbook at `excel/MDAR_Converter.xlsx` and staged for web download at `public/downloads/MDAR_Converter.xlsx`.

## Key Features
1. **Zero Macros (Macro-Free):** 100% native OpenXML formulas, no VBA code, no external link dependencies, completely safe for institutional/enterprise environments.
2. **9 Specialized Sheets:**
   - `00_Home`: Index, platform overview, security statements, compatibility notes.
   - `01_DD_DMS_DDM`: Bidirectional angle conversions with degrees/minutes/seconds and formatted text.
   - `02_Single_Point`: Interactive single point coordinate calculator for WGS 84, UTM Zone 36N, and Egypt Red Belt.
   - `03_Batch_Converter`: Tabular multi-row coordinate converter for survey points.
   - `04_Egypt_ETM`: Helmert 1906 ellipsoid parameters, Molodensky-Badekas 10-parameter shifting vectors, and 4 ETM Belts.
   - `05_UTM_WGS84`: Universal Transverse Mercator central meridian formula `6*Zone - 183` and zones 1–60.
   - `06_CRS_Catalog`: Curated geodetic catalog of 197 coordinate systems with PROJ4 strings.
   - `07_Accuracy_Validation`: Ground truth control points (Cairo, Alexandria, Makkah, Dubai) with delta residual checks.
   - `08_Documentation`: Mathematical derivations, EPSG citations, and usage constraints.
